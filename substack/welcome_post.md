# Welcome to The Daily Macro & Market Brief

Every day, hours of market commentary, dozens of data releases, and countless expert opinions compete for your attention. Most of it is noise.

I started this newsletter because I needed it myself. Working in macro at a fund means synthesizing vast amounts of information—economic data, central bank communications, market moves, geopolitical developments—into coherent views. The challenge: keeping up with everything that matters is a full-time job on top of your actual job.

**The Daily Macro & Market Brief** solves that problem. Every weekday, I cut through the noise and deliver what actually moved markets in a format you can read in 5 minutes.

---

## What You'll Get

**Daily (Mon-Fri):**
- Key economic data with context—what was expected, why it matters
- Market moves across equities, rates, FX, and commodities
- Central bank signals and policy developments
- Facts first—you draw the conclusions

**Weekly (Fridays):**
- Synthesis of the week's major themes
- How narratives evolved day-to-day
- Key risks and catalysts ahead

---

## Why Subscribe

Markets reward those who do the work to understand what's happening. Most people skim headlines. Few systematically track the data, the policy signals, the cross-asset moves.

Whether you're in finance, macro research, policy, consulting, or just intellectually curious about how the world works—consistent attention to what matters compounds.

No paywalls. No affiliate links. No positions to pump. Just the macro story, clearly told.
