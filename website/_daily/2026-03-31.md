**Energy & Geopolitics**

Oil markets whipsawed on conflicting signals from the Iran conflict, with diplomatic hopes quickly undermined by escalating attacks.

- Brent crude traded around $107-113/barrel, WTI at $102/barrel - both well above pre-war levels despite pullback on ceasefire speculation
- Iran struck Kuwaiti tanker off Dubai hours after WSJ reported Trump considering exit from conflict without securing Strait of Hormuz reopening
- 10 million barrels per day supply disruption continues from Hormuz closure, with only trickle of traffic negotiating passage
- Power outages reported in eastern Iran following explosions, suggesting ground reality diverging from diplomatic rhetoric
- Houthis facing Iranian pressure to renew Red Sea attacks, which could push oil to $140-160/barrel if Saudi Yanbu outlet compromised
- US gasoline reached $4.02/gallon - first time above $4 since August 2022, up from ~$2.98 pre-conflict

**Inflation & Central Banks**

Central banks face impossible policy choices as energy shocks drive inflation above targets while growth risks mount.

- Fed Chair Powell described inflation pass-through as potentially "transitory," calming bond markets
- Inflation expectations remain "well anchored beyond short term" according to Powell, but March CPI preview at 3.4% (+100bps from recent lows)
- Eurozone CPI jumped to 2.5% YoY in March - largest increase since 2022, with Germany accelerating faster than expected at 2.6%
- Rate cuts now off table for 2025 given expected inflation surge; ECB pricing two 25bp hikes while BoE facing similar pressure
- UK inflation at 4.5% on 2-year zero-coupon swaps - 250bps above 2% target after missing target for five years
- Fed independence faces political pressure with Kevin Warsh nomination looming

**Asia Pacific & China**

China showed surprising resilience while regional markets suffered from energy vulnerability and tech sector rotation.

- China Manufacturing PMI beat at 50.4 vs 50.1 expected - highest in 12 months, with Non-Manufacturing at 50.1 vs 49.9 expected
- Asia equities initially down 2-3%, recovered to flat on Trump exit report, then faded again on tanker strike
- China A-shares relatively flat; Moutai +4% on first price hike in three years; Midea +3.8% on tech pivot
- Korea down 17% month-to-date, most exposed to Hormuz closure plus memory chip narrative deteriorating
- Global long-only funds turned neutral on China equities in February - first time since 2022

**Technology & Memory Chips**

Memory sector faced new disruption from Google's breakthrough efficiency technology, shifting vulnerability from HBM to flash storage.

- SOX Index flat year-to-date with Nvidia down 20% from October peak into bear territory
- Google Turbo Quant technology (6x memory reduction) causing sector reassessment
- Flash/NAND now seen as more vulnerable than high-bandwidth memory (HBM)
- Micron plunged double-digits; Kioxia particularly exposed despite hedge fund enthusiasm
- Memory chip disruption adding to tech sector rotation away from growth stocks

**Emerging Markets**

Vulnerable economies face demand destruction without fiscal capacity to absorb energy shock.

- Philippines inflation forecast jumped to 3.9% upper bound - up 1.5pp from February's 2.4%
- Stockpiles down to ~45 days in Philippines, highlighting supply chain stress
- Africa fuel shortages emerging across continent, hitting poorest economies with limited FX reserves
- Stagflation risks mounting in countries without fiscal buffers to subsidize energy prices

**Fixed Income & FX**

Bond markets showed technical resilience despite inflation pressures, while dollar strength faced headwinds from policy uncertainty.

- 10-year Treasury yield down 3bps; Japan 10-year down 10bps to 3.69% despite energy vulnerability
- Bond vigilantes effectively tightening financial conditions for Fed
- Dollar gains capped despite safe-haven status - trust damaged by policy volatility
- Swiss franc suppressed by SNB intervention; yen weak on energy import dependency
- Gold above $4,500 for third consecutive session - one of few safe havens working
