DAILY BRIEF:
Major US-Iran interim deal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz by Friday drove a broad risk rally, with oil falling to three-month lows and Asian equities posting their strongest two-day gain since March 2022. The agreement includes a 60-day negotiation window on nuclear issues but faces implementation uncertainty and Israeli resistance. Fed Chair Worsh's first meeting Wednesday now confronts reduced inflation pressure from energy relief but persistent core stickiness.

**Oil & Energy**

WTI and Brent crude plunged on expectations the strait will reopen within days, though significant operational hurdles remain.

- WTI fell 5.6% to $80/barrel, Brent down 5.2% to $82-84 - both at lowest levels since early March
- December oil contracts also declined sharply, suggesting markets expect sustained supply relief
- European natural gas dropped 5% on sentiment despite limited direct impact
- Global oil inventories remain at critical levels after 15 weeks of drawdowns during the blockade
- US Strategic Petroleum Reserve at 348 million barrels, approaching operational minimum of 270 million
- Qatar's Ras Laffan LNG facility remains 17% offline, limiting immediate supply normalization

**Equities & Risk Assets**

Asian markets led a global relief rally, though questions persist about sustainability given deal fragility.

- MSCI Asia Pacific gained 3.2%, best two-day performance since March 2022
- Nikkei 225 closed at record high; Korea's Kospi surged 5.3%
- Philippines equities rose 3% (best day in five years), Indonesia opened up 3%
- Euro Stoxx 50 futures advanced 1.8%, US futures up 1-2% across indices
- Equal-weight S&P 500 and Russell 2000 hit records Friday while cap-weighted indices remain below June 2 highs
- Airlines outperformed on jet fuel expectations; Korean Air jumped 13%
- Oil majors declined 6-7% including PetroChina and CNOOC
- SpaceX continued gains from Friday's IPO debut, up 6% pre-market to extend 19% Friday surge

**Fixed Income & FX**

Bond markets rallied but less than oil moves would historically suggest, while emerging market currencies strengthened broadly.

- 10-year Treasury yield fell 4.5 basis points to 4.44%, 2-year down 5-6bp to 4.02-4.04%
- JPMorgan analysts note 10-year should trade 420-430bp based on historical oil correlations
- Fed rate hike pricing declined to 16bp by year-end from 25bp pre-deal
- Market now sees 60-75% probability of at least one Fed hike versus 80% Friday
- Dollar weakened across emerging markets; Korean won and Asian currencies strengthened
- USD/JPY held above 160 despite dollar weakness and BOJ hike expectations

**Central Bank Week**

Multiple central bank decisions this week face shifting inflation dynamics from energy relief that may take months to materialize.

- Fed (Wednesday): Worsh's first meeting expected to hold rates and remove easing bias; potential for first unanimous decision in months
- Bank of Japan (Tuesday): 25bp hike expected to highest level since 1995 despite Governor Ueda's absence for surgery recovery
- Bank Indonesia: 50bp hike expected despite off-cycle move days ago as credibility remains at stake
- Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas: 50bp hike likely as inflation concerns persist despite oil relief

**Deal Implementation Risks**

The interim agreement faces significant hurdles that could derail relief expectations within the 60-day negotiation window.

- No deal text released; conflicting US-Iran statements on terms including frozen funds and sanctions sequencing
- Iran demands 5-day pause in Israeli operations in Lebanon, which has not occurred
- Israeli National Security Minister rejected deal constraints on Hezbollah operations
- Trump threatened renewed military strikes if Iran doesn't dismantle nuclear infrastructure during talks
- Strait reopening timeline estimated 30-60 days minimum for mine clearing and insurance restoration
- Bloomberg Economics assesses low probability of lasting peace

**Technology & AI**

AI sector faced regulatory intervention while broader tech names benefited from risk rally.

- US ordered shutdown of Anthropic's most advanced AI models (Fable 5, Mythos) for foreign nationals after "jailbreak" discovery
- DeepSeek downgraded to neutral by JPMorgan on monetization concerns
- Zhipu upgraded on better pricing power expectations
- Taiwan industrial production surged 29% year-over-year as economy shows overheating signs with 10% growth expected
