DAILY BRIEF:
US-Iran peace negotiations showed concrete progress as President Trump declared the "war is over" and claimed a deal could be signed this weekend in Europe. SpaceX completed its record $75 billion IPO at a $1.7 trillion valuation amid 4x oversubscription, though governance concerns emerged over unprecedented founder control structures. The ECB delivered its first rate hike in three years while signaling additional tightening ahead as inflation broadens beyond energy.

**Geopolitics & Energy**

Iran peace deal momentum accelerated despite lack of formal confirmation from Tehran. Semi-official Iranian media reported a 14-point draft agreement including oil sanctions relief and Strait of Hormuz reopening within 30 days.

- Brent crude fell 4.5% to $86 (WTI December at $82) on de-escalation optimism
- European gas futures declined 7% on potential supply normalization
- VIX dropped below 19 from elevated war-risk levels
- Goldman cut 2027 oil forecast by $5 to low-$80s range

Key execution risks remain with the Strait of Hormuz still closed and Israel-Lebanon tensions at 25-year highs creating third-party complications.

**Equities & IPOs**

Asian markets rallied broadly on geopolitical relief, though structural concerns persist around market dynamics and valuations.

- MSCI Asia-Pacific gained 2.6% (earlier +3.5%)
- Korean KOSPI surged 7-8%, with YTD gains now approaching 90%
- Leveraged ETF flows drove 60-70% of SK Hynix trading volume
- Korean won led Asian FX gains as foreign investors returned to local equities

SpaceX's $135 billion IPO became the largest ever with retail orders exceeding $100 billion for just 20% allocation. However, governance structure raised institutional concerns with Musk holding over 1 billion shares at 10x voting power and appointing himself CEO for life with sole removal authority.

**Central Bank Policy**

The ECB delivered a 25bp hike while projecting higher inflation across baseline scenarios, signaling the hawkish pivot extends beyond emergency energy measures.

- Services inflation remains "stubborn at 3.5%" according to ECB officials
- Bundesbank's Nagel indicated July hike likely if necessary
- Markets now price 50bp additional ECB tightening this year
- Bank of Korea's Shin warned rates "need to be raised before too late"

Fed expectations shifted toward cuts as officials view policy already in restrictive territory, assuming oil shock fades and tariff effects diminish.

**China & Regulation**

Chinese regulators summoned seven major internet platforms over "misleading promotions," though market reaction remained muted suggesting desensitization to enforcement actions.

- Alibaba, JD.com, Tencent gained 1%+ despite regulatory warnings
- CSI 300 rose 1.3%, ChiNext advanced 3%
- MSCI China approaches bear market territory (-20% threshold)
- May retail sales consensus at -0.3% month-over-month (release June 16)

Enforcement appears measured with warnings rather than fines, targeting broader market behavior over specific company crackdowns.

**Commodities & Currencies**

Energy complex sold off on peace deal optimism while precious metals stabilized above key levels.

- Oil curve through year-end remains at $85-90, suggesting limited further downside
- Gold held above $4,100 after earlier weakness
- Chow Tai Fook surged 12-15% on earnings beat

European fiscal constraints intensified with UK Defense Secretary resigning over £20 billion funding shortfall, highlighting tension between 5% NATO spending targets and elevated borrowing costs.

**Technology & AI**

Semiconductor rally continued on SpaceX IPO liquidity effects, though valuation concerns persist across AI infrastructure plays.

- MediaTek gained on 170% YTD surge, targeting record quarter as revenue mix shifts from smartphones (50%) to hyperscaler AI chips
- Oracle's capex guidance disappointed, with analysts warning of potential "big selloff" after upcoming Anthropic/OpenAI IPOs
- Tech sector concentration heading toward 50%+ of S&P 500 by end-2027

**Risks & Outlook**

Multiple catalysts converge over the weekend including potential Iran deal signing and Swiss referendum on 10 million population cap that could trigger EU relationship crisis. Korean market structure shows stress signals with VIX equivalent at 90 despite strong year-to-date performance, driven by leveraged products creating potential forced deleveraging risk. SpaceX governance precedent may accelerate similar founder-control structures across upcoming IPOs, with law firms already pitching copycat arrangements to clients.
